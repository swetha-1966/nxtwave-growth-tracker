# nxtwave-growth-tracker
# NxtWave Growth Challenge – AI Workshop Referral Tracker

## Overview

This project was created for the **NxtWave Growth Intern – Growth Challenge**.

The challenge was to plan a campaign to get **500 final-year engineering students** to register for the free online workshop:

**Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes**

The campaign was designed for **7 days** with a budget of **₹2,000**.

The main goal was to create a low-cost student acquisition strategy supported by a functional referral-tracking prototype.

---

## Target Audience

The primary target audience is:

- Final-year engineering students
- CSE, IT, AI/ML and Data Science students
- Students preparing for placements
- Students interested in building practical AI projects

### Why would they care?

The workshop provides students with an opportunity to:

- Build an AI project in just 60 minutes
- Gain practical exposure to AI
- Add a project experience to their profile
- Learn something useful for placements
- Attend the workshop for free

---

## Growth Strategy

The campaign focuses on four main channels:

| Channel | Expected Registrations |
|---|---:|
| College clubs & student communities | 200 |
| WhatsApp student groups | 150 |
| Student referral campaign | 100 |
| Email & student communities | 50 |
| **Total** | **500** |

The strategy prioritizes organic and community-based growth instead of depending heavily on paid advertising because the available budget is only ₹2,000.

---

## 7-Day Campaign Plan

### Day 1
- Finalize the workshop message
- Set up the registration flow
- Prepare referral tracking

### Day 2
- Start outreach through college communities
- Share the workshop registration message

### Day 3
- Monitor registrations
- Start student referral campaign
- Identify the best-performing channels

### Day 4
- Increase outreach through the best-performing channels
- Encourage more students to share referral codes

### Day 5
- Push the referral campaign
- Share reminders in student communities

### Day 6
- Send registration reminders
- Focus on channels generating the highest conversions

### Day 7
- Final registration push
- Use urgency and workshop reminders to reach the 500 target

# Working Asset – AI Workshop Referral Tracker

The main asset created for this campaign is an **AI Workshop Referral Tracker**.

### How it works

1. A student registers for the workshop.
2. The system generates a unique referral code.
3. The student shares the referral code with friends.
4. New registrations can be associated with the referral code.
5. The dashboard tracks registration and referral activity.
6. A leaderboard shows the top referrers.

### Dashboard Metrics

The prototype displays:

- Total registrations
- Target of 500 registrations
- Referral registrations
- Active referrers
- Campaign progress
- Top referrers
- Recent registrations

---

## Key Growth Tests

### Test 1 – College Community Outreach

**Hypothesis:** Students are more likely to register when the workshop is shared through trusted college clubs and student communities.

**Metric:** Registration conversion rate and registrations generated per community.

### Test 2 – Referral Campaign

**Hypothesis:** Students will invite friends when they receive a simple referral code and can track their progress.

**Metric:** Referral registrations per student and percentage of registrations generated through referrals.

### Test 3 – Messaging / CTA

**Hypothesis:** A clear message focused on building an AI project in 60 minutes will generate more registrations than a generic workshop message.

**Metric:** Click-to-registration conversion rate.

## AI Usage

AI tools were used during the planning and building process for:

- Generating and comparing growth strategies
- Selecting the most suitable campaign channels
- Designing the referral workflow
- Improving the workshop messaging
- Structuring the dashboard
- Developing the HTML, CSS and JavaScript prototype

AI suggestions were evaluated rather than followed blindly. The final decisions were based on the campaign budget, target audience and 500-registration goal.

---

## AI Suggestion Rejected

AI suggested using paid advertising as one of the main ways to reach the 500-registration target.

This was deliberately rejected as the primary strategy because the campaign budget was only **₹2,000**.

Instead, the campaign focuses on:

- College communities
- WhatsApp groups
- Student referrals
- Organic student networks

These approaches are more suitable for a limited-budget student-focused campaign.

---

## Important Note

This is a **functional prototype/demo** created for the NxtWave Growth Challenge.

The current version uses browser `LocalStorage` for storing registration data. Therefore, data is stored locally in the browser and is **not synchronized across different users or devices**.

A production version could use a backend database and authentication to support real-time multi-user registration and referral tracking.
